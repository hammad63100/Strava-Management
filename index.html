A Model Built on Alignment.
The quality of an Amazon business is shaped by the quality of the decisions made before and after launch.

PHILOSOPHY

Experienced Oversight. Direct Accountability.

Experienced oversight remains close to the market thesis, unit economics, capital allocation, inventory position, and operating performance. The founder receives judgment, not merely reporting.


Capital Deserves Discipline

Not every product should be launched. Not every market should be entered. Not every increase in advertising expenditure creates value.
We challenge assumptions before resources are committed. Where the economics are weak, we revise the thesis or recommend that the founder does not proceed.
Capital preservation is part of stewardship — and it is what protects the founder's ability to take the next opportunity when it appears.


A Capped Roster

Strava Management works with a limited number of portfolio brands at any one time.
This protects the standard of experienced attention, operating review, and commercial judgment required by each founder-partner.

The Vendor and Partner Gap

  • Completes assigned tasks.

  • Reports activity and channel metrics.

    Reports revenue quality, profit, capital needs, and risk.

  • Optimizes separate functions.

    Connects formation, market selection, activation, and management.

  • Responds to the current month.

    Builds toward the founder's long-term objective.

  • Sells a defined scope of hours.

    Accepts accountability for the operating plan.

We would rather build fewer assets well.


Transparent Alignment


Before an operating partnership begins, we establish the founder's objective, available capital, ownership horizon, risk tolerance, and expected level of involvement.
The operating plan is then built around those realities.

THE PROCESS

A Disciplined Sequence from Formation to Asset Value.

THE PROCESS

A Disciplined Sequence from Formation to Asset Value.

  • Phase 01 — Foundation & Company Formation
    “ Is the business structured to operate correctly? ”

    “ We establish the legal and operating foundation before marketplace activity begins. Specialist legal, tax, banking, or compliance work may be delivered by qualified third parties where required. ”

    • ● Founder objective and ownership structure
    • ● US company formation coordination — open to founders anywhere in the world, regardless of citizenship or residency
    • ● EIN and foundational documentation guidance
    • ● Business banking and payment-readiness coordination, including remote account opening for international founders
    • ● Amazon seller account preparation
    • ● Trademark and brand-protection pathway
    • ● Operating budget and launch roadmap
  • Phase 02 — Market Selection & Validation
    “ Is there a credible market worth entering? ”

    “ We look for a defendable commercial gap, not simply a product with high search volume. ”

    • ● Category demand and maturity
    • ● Competitive concentration
    • ● Customer expectations and review barriers
    • ● Pricing architecture
    • ● Keyword and search behaviour
    • ● Differentiation potential
    • ● Regulatory and supply-chain exposure
    • ● Portfolio expansion potential
  • Phase 03 — Viability Assessment
    “ Can the opportunity produce a durable return? ”

    “ The founder receives a clear recommendation: proceed, revise, or decline. ”

    • ● Landed-cost modelling
    • ● Amazon fee structure
    • ● Contribution-margin forecast
    • ● Customer acquisition assumptions
    • ● Inventory and working-capital requirements
    • ● Break-even analysis
    • ● Sensitivity testing
    • ● Launch-capital requirements
  • Phase 04 — Brand Development & Activation
    “ Can the brand communicate and convert? ”

    “ We build the conversion infrastructure required for a disciplined market entry. ”

    • ● Brand positioning and naming coordination
    • ● Packaging and visual direction
    • ● Supplier and sample coordination
    • ● Search-led product-page strategy
    • ● Product photography and image direction
    • ● Copy, SEO, and enhanced brand content
    • ● Pricing and offer architecture
    • ● Initial inventory and launch sequencing
  • Phase 05 — Performance Stewardship
    “ Can the business expand without weakening the economics? ”

    “ Spend is increased when the economics justify it. Revenue is not treated as success in isolation. ”

    • ● Advertising and demand stewardship
    • ● Organic search position
    • ● Conversion performance
    • ● Pricing integrity
    • ● Inventory oversight
    • ● Contribution margin
    • ● Competitive movement
    • ● Account health and marketplace compliance
    • ● Product-level reporting
    • ● Expansion decisions
  • Phase 06 — Asset Enhancement & Exit-Readiness
    “ Is the business becoming more transferable and valuable? ”

    “ Exit-readiness strengthens the company even when the founder intends to retain long-term ownership. ”

    • ● Financial clarity
    • ● Documented operating procedures
    • ● Supplier resilience
    • ● Brand ownership
    • ● Inventory controls
    • ● Founder independence
    • ● Portfolio diversification
    • ● Reliable earnings history
    • ● Transaction-readiness documentation
